# CLAUDE.md

## Design skills — say "use apple skills"

`.claude/skills/` carries Apple's design standard (copied from aeroaswar/general):

- `apple-skills` — the one trigger. Say **"use apple skills"** (or `/apple-skills`); it routes to the right skill below, lists its guideline decisions before building, and reviews as What / Why / Fix.
- `apple-look` — Apple's Human Interface Guidelines (123 pages) for **apps**; for websites only its typography, color and accessibility pages apply.
- `apple-motion` — Apple's WWDC fluid-motion rules for the **web**: springs, press feedback, interruption, velocity, reduced motion.
