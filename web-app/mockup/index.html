One list, one direction. A quote becomes an order when it is accepted, which issues the invoice; payment moves the order into packing; dispatch deducts stock; delivery closes it and sets the next reorder. Nothing here is typed — every row is derived from the record as it stands.

VariantContentPriceOn handReservedAvailable

On hand is the balance of the movement ledger, not a stored figure. A sent quote and an order not yet dispatched hold their lines; dispatch takes them off the ledger; expiry, loss and cancellation release them. Nothing available flags the variant as a stockout. Tap a variant to record a movement.
