# Completed stone catalogue and footer

All 72 shop products now have their own stone photograph, used consistently in the catalogue and detail pages. The set comprises 65 compounds, three devices and four apparel products. The homepage and other collections use the same compound image mapping.

The footer has a plain charcoal field, one small AXIOM wordmark, aligned links and compact spacing. The photographic stone band above it fades through a CSS gradient into the page background without the former hard vertical edge.

Image masters, exact generation records and optimized WebP assets are saved locally. See QA.md for verification and the scope of the demo.
