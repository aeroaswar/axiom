Sheet density

Adds a magenta cut contour on the trim, 1 mm of bleed, and a spec line on each page. Turn off for printing at home.

Black on white, one label per page, for a direct-thermal roll printer such as the Xprinter XP-420B or XP-D4601B (203 dpi). Load 50 × 30 mm labels, one across the roll with a gap between them. Use top-coated synthetic direct-thermal labels: plain thermal paper smears under an alcohol swab and fades with heat and sunlight. Set the same 50 × 30 mm size in the printer driver and calibrate the gap sensor.

One 50 × 30 mm page per label, nothing else on the page. Colour is the AXIOM label for a print shop or for sharing; black & white is the same design as the thermal roll.

Before a roll. Print one label first and check Research use only, the smallest line. If it breaks up, raise the printer's darkness (density) a step, then slow the print speed. Keep labelled vials out of heat and direct sun: direct-thermal print darkens or fades under both. The thermal version drops the bronze and the onyx ground: it is the working label, not the brand-book stock. A label may not imply human dose or frequency; the DOSE row is a pen-device calibration and is yours to leave off.
