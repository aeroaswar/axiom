Vial sticker — 20 × 40 mm

Wrap label for pen-format research peptides, drawn in the AXIOM system: onyx ground, bone ink, a single bronze hairline and one bronze mark. Geometry is declared in millimetres, so what is on screen is what comes off the press. Retatrutide 30 mg is the worked example.

01

The label

Shown at five times actual size. Every element below is the same markup the print sheet emits — not a redrawing.

02

Actual size

1:1. A browser maps CSS millimetres through a nominal 96 dpi, so this is exact only on a 96 dpi display — treat it as indicative and measure a printed proof with a ruler before signing off.

03

Corner radius

The die radius, at three times size. 2 mm is the default and what the print sheet ships with; change --r on .lbl to pick another after a test print. Shown 1 / 1.5 / 2 / 2.5 mm.

04

Across the catalogue

The label is data-driven, so the rest of the range drops in without redrawing. A long compound name shrinks its own band rather than crossing the die line, and a SKU with no click conversion supplied simply loses the DOSE row.

06

Production notes

Stock. The brand book specifies this label as cold-foil bone-white on matte black polypropylene with soft-touch matte lamination. The print sheet renders the ground as a solid ink flood so it can be proofed on white stock, which is heavy and unreliable on an office inkjet. For production, send the PDF to the vendor and print on black stock.
Die-cut files. Set --gutter-x and --gutter-y to 0 in the print sheet to butt the labels together for a kiss-cut roll; the hairline cut guides hide themselves when the gutters close.
Micro-print. Research use only sets at 4.0 pt. That is normal for pharma micro-print but it is the one line to check on a real proof — if the press cannot hold it, raise .lbl__ruo to 1.6 mm and drop the compound to 3.6 mm.
Label copy. The brand's own label rule states that a label may not imply human dose, route or frequency. DOSE 10 clicks = 1 mg is a pen-device calibration rather than a human dose, and is carried here as specified — it lives in the data layer, so setting dose to null on a SKU removes the row entirely.
