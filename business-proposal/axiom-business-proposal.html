01 Executive summary

One standard, five pillars, a decade-long market.

The opportunity is a serious audience underserved by a loud category — and a single standard of verification that turns compliance into the moat.

Indonesia's performance and longevity buyers — researchers, career athletes, recovery studios and people who train for decades — are served today by neon marketing and unverifiable claims. AXIOM answers with the opposite posture: research-grade peptides verified by HPLC/MS to ≥ 98% with a Certificate of Analysis per lot, clinical red-light hardware at true 660 + 850 nm, and a restraint that reads as credibility rather than caution.

The plan opens on the smallest viable market — the Decade Athlete and the Clinic Buyer — and carries growth through devices, wellness and apparel while peptides remain strictly Research Use Only and B2B-research. Direct WhatsApp commerce, cold-chain dispatch and documentation in every box compound trust into reorders.

The model is capital-efficient and margin-honest: a blended gross margin near 54.5%, three funded scenarios, and a Year-1 base case of Rp 4.800.000.000. Regulatory posture is treated as risk number one and engineered into every surface — an acknowledgement gate, jurisdiction caution and a verbatim RUO disclaimer wherever peptides appear.

What follows is three phases: a strategy pass from an eight-person advisory board, a business model with unit economics and financials, and a phased launch roadmap with dated milestones and a KPI dashboard. The through-line is a single sentence: documented, not promised.

02 Phase A · Strategy

The board, in their lanes — then one reconciled plan.

Eight operators, each on the part of the business they know coldest. Every offer, script and campaign is written to clear the compliance line: no dosing, no medical claim, no superlative.

Alex Hormozi Offer engineering Grand Slam Offer per pillar

Build one irresistible offer per pillar by assembling the value so the documentation does the persuading. The value equation for this audience is trust and the long run — never an outcome. The risk reversal commits to verification and documentation, not health outcomes.

  • Peptide Science (RUO) — research-grade material with CoA per lot, HPLC/MS ≥ 98%, cold-chain dispatch, lot-level traceability. Risk reversal: CoA in every box or your money back. Research Use Only, in-vitro.
  • Therapy & Recovery — spec-honest hardware, verified irradiance at 660 + 850 nm, a 30-day return window and a supply commitment for clinic accounts.
  • Wellness — documented sourcing, third-party identity testing, subscription reorder at a member price.
  • Longevity — compounds documented to the same standard as the peptide line, with the same verification paperwork.
  • Apparel — a quiet identity range with a lifetime seam warranty and free exchange.

The value assembly across pillars is deliberately un-hyped: the offer is the paperwork.

Dan Kennedy Direct-response · high-ticket B2B Clinic Buyer & Researcher motion

The high-ticket money is B2B. Two motions carry it:

  • Spec-sheet sales letters for the Clinic Buyer — a two-page document leading with verified irradiance, warranty terms, restock reliability and total cost over the relationship, closing on a demo booking.
  • Quote-to-close on WhatsApp for the Researcher — an itemised IDR quote with live stock, purity data and CoA sample attached, moving to a documented order.

Systematise the back end: a reorder rhythm that triggers a message before a lab or clinic runs dry, a magnetic promise of supply and documentation, and a house account for repeat B2B buyers. The offer competes on certainty, not price.

Seth Godin Positioning · smallest viable market Documented, not promised

Do not open to everyone. The first tribe is the Decade Athlete and the Clinic Buyer — people who already distrust the neon and are looking for a calm, clinical system to commit to for years.

The purple cow is a posture, not a product: documented, not promised. In a category of overclaim, the remarkable thing is the restraint — the CoA, the lot number, the honest spec. That is the story that spreads without a single claim, because it makes the buyer look discerning for choosing it.

Grant Cardone Targets & sales cadence The number, then the math

The 12-month target is Rp 4.800.000.000 in the base case — roughly Rp 400.000.000 per month at run-rate. Work it backward into daily activity, then multiply the pipeline (not the claims) to protect it.

  • Average documented order value blends to about Rp 1.100.000 across pillars.
  • Base run-rate needs roughly 360 paid orders per month — near 14 per working day.
  • At a quote-to-close rate of 32%, that is about 44 qualified quotes per working day across WhatsApp and B2B.
  • Sales cadence: 44 quotes out, 60 follow-ups, 20 reorder prompts daily — logged, measured, never skipped.

Ten times the outreach, the follow-up and the reorder discipline. The claims stay flat and factual.

Gary Vaynerchuk Content engine Document, don't promise

Run an organic engine that documents the standard instead of selling an outcome. Every asset is proof: a CoA on the bench, a lab macro of a purity read, a recovery scene shot in natural light, a lot number being sealed.

  • Daily — one proof frame (CoA, spec, cold-chain) on the primary channel.
  • Weekly — one long-form teardown (how a lot is verified, what irradiance really means) repurposed into short clips and carousels.
  • Monthly — one persona story: a clinic fit-out, a researcher's documentation workflow, a decade athlete's calm routine.

The cadence compounds because it is honest and repeatable — jab with proof, and the ask is quiet.

Tony Robbins Founder operating rhythm Built for the long run

A decade build needs a decade rhythm. Protect the founder's energy and decision quality with a fixed weekly operating cadence.

  • Monday — numbers review: pipeline, reorders, CoA turnaround, cash. Set the week's one priority.
  • Daily — a 90-minute deep block on the priority before any messages; a physical training block to model the market being served.
  • Wednesday — customer hour: read every conversation, answer three personally.
  • Friday — decision routine: reversible calls made fast, irreversible ones (entity, capital, whether peptides ship) slowed and documented.
  • Monthly — a full day away from the desk to protect judgment over the long run.

Supply chain

Sourcing & verification

Peptide material is sourced from vetted synthesis partners, then routed through an independent HPLC/MS verification pipeline that confirms identity and purity to ≥ 98% and issues a Certificate of Analysis per lot before any lot is released.

  • Verification partner — third-party HPLC/MS with a target CoA turnaround under 5 working days.
  • Lot control — every lot numbered, documented and traceable end to end.
Cold-chain

Storage & transit

Research material is held at −20 °C in monitored storage and moved at 2–8 °C in insured, tracked transit with temperature logging in the box.

  • Storage — −20 °C, alarmed, redundant power.
  • Transit — 2–8 °C, phase-change packs, documentation enclosed.
Devices & apparel

QC & production

Red-light hardware is sourced against a spec and QC-checked for verified irradiance at 660 + 850 nm before dispatch. Apparel is produced to a neutral, precise line with seam and print checks.

  • Device QC — spectral and irradiance read per unit batch.
  • Apparel — line production, exchange window, quiet identity.
