# Pricing structure — rebuild proposal

The catalogue is priced by a 48% cost-plus formula and then hand-corrected wherever
a competitor turned up. This documents what those corrections have in common, and
two structures that would absorb them into rules.

Modelled from `data/axiom-price-list-full.csv` and the "BP" competitor list.
Cost basis = supplier lot + Rp 600.000 throughout.

## Diagnosis

| | |
|---|---:|
| Lots priced by hand | 11 of 81 |
| Of those, contested (a competitor sells the compound) | **11 of 11** |
| Contested lots still priced on formula | 13 |
| Gross-margin spread | 16.7% – 78.0% |

**There is a second pricing rule and it has never been written down.** Where nobody
competes, price comes from cost. Where somebody competes, price comes from their
price — *up* for GHK-Cu and NAD+, *down* for the other nine. Both are defensible
rules. The problem is that only one is written, so the other gets applied from
memory, to some lots and not others: **13 contested lots are still priced as though
no competitor existed**, including every Retatrutide and Tirzepatide lot.

**Both proposed structures raise all six.** A hard floor is the single change that
does most of the work.

## Two structures

Both share the same shape — cost-plus by default, market-anchored where a competitor
exists, a hard 40% floor underneath. They differ on one question: **when is a
competitor's lot actually a substitute for yours?**

### Structure A — substitution

A competitor lot counts only if it is within **0.7–1.4×** your lot size. A 10 mg vial
is not a substitute for a 32 mg pen, so it prices from cost.

- Revenue Rp 257.800.000 · gross margin 48.3%
- Per-lot range 40.4% – 62.7% · 13 lots move

### Structure B — per-unit optics

Buyers read price per mg whatever the lot size, so any competitor reference sets a
ceiling. Closer to what has actually been happening — but applied to all 24 contested
lots rather than the 11 that came up.

- Revenue Rp 256.700.000 · gross margin 48.1%
- Per-lot range 40.0% – 78.8% · 22 lots move

**Both earn more than today.** A adds Rp 2.500.000 of revenue, B adds Rp 1.400.000,
because raising the six sub-floor lots outweighs the cuts each makes elsewhere.
Today's book is Rp 255.300.000 at 47.8%.

## Why the ceiling keeps binding

**AXIOM sells 10–20 unit lots against 32–100 unit pens.** The Rp 600.000
kit-and-handling block is charged once per lot, so the competitor spreads it over
three to ten times more material. On a 10 mg vial that block is 40% of the price; on
their 32 mg pen it is a rounding error. **No margin rule closes a gap that is
arithmetic.** Matching the format does:

| Melanotan II 20 mg | 1.500.000 | 2.100.000 | 4.000.000 | 2.750.000 | +45% |
| Tesamorelin 32 mg | 2.560.000 | 3.160.000 | 6.100.000 | 3.900.000 | +56% |

**Two are free wins.** A 60 mg Tirzepatide lands 21% under the competitor at full
margin, a 32 mg MOTS-c 6% under. Neither needs a discount, a rule change or a new
supplier — only a lot size not currently listed. The other four stay dear even at
matched format, which is the re-sourcing case restated in cost terms.

## Recommendation

**1 · Put in the 40% floor now.** One line of policy. It costs nothing — it *adds*
revenue — and it is the only change that prevents the next ad-hoc cut. Worth doing
whether or not either structure is adopted.

**2 · Adopt Structure A.** It earns more (Rp 2.500.000 against B's Rp 1.400.000),
moves fewer lots, and rests on a claim defensible to a customer: a 10 mg vial and a
32 mg pen are different products. Structure B concedes a comparison that flatters the
competitor and then pays for it across 22 lots. If buyers genuinely do shop on
price-per-mg regardless of format, take B — but take it whole, not on the eleven lots
that happened to come up.

**3 · Add the 60 mg Tirzepatide and the 32 mg MOTS-c.** Both beat the competitor at
full margin. This is the only lever that changes the per-unit comparison instead of
paying for it.

## Not changed

Nothing in this document has been applied. `AXIOM-Price-List-Full.pdf` and its data
files still carry today's prices, including all eleven hand-set lots.
