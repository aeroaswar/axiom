The structure

What the book actually earns. And what the pen is.

Every lot is a vial from R-Peptides plus a Reusable Injection Pen V2 from R-Peptides. Both are goods on the invoice, so what remains is a true gross margin — and the pen is the same Rp 600.000 on every lot, whatever the vial inside costs.

The pen is a good, not a service. That distinction decides the entire architecture. Because it is one physical pen per lot, a multi-lot order carries no shared cost that can be given away, and a returning buyer who already owns a pen still receives another. There is no free discount anywhere in this structure. Every rupiah taken off a price comes out of real margin.

02
The rule

The leader brings them in. The pair carries the margin.

One sentence governs every promotion in the house, and it follows directly from the shape of the book.

  1. A leader is never discounted. BPC-157, TB-500, Tesamorelin 10 mg, Melanotan II and Retatrutide 10 mg are already below the floor. They earn their place by pulling the price-shopper in at a number no documented competitor will match. Taking anything off them converts a thin sale into a loss.
  2. A leader is never quoted alone. If a first conversation ends with one leader in the basket, the house has bought a customer and sold nothing. The quote always carries a second lot beside it.
  3. The discount is funded by the second lot, not the first. Room lives in the headroom five and in the thin slice each standard lot holds above the floor.
  4. Margin is tested on the blended order, never per line. A pair at 43,1% is a good order even though half of it sits at 21,9%. A line-by-line rule would forbid the very trade that makes the leader worth carrying.
  5. The floor is 40% blended. Below it, the order is re-cut or declined — it is not approved as an exception because the buyer pushed.

This is why the discount conversation is not a pricing conversation. The number a buyer sees is set by what else is in the order, which is a question the house controls and the buyer does not.

03
Mechanics

The promotion ladder. Reach for these in order.

Because no discount here is free, the mechanics that cost nothing come first and the ones that cost margin come last. The order is the discipline.

Rung 1 costs nothing · rung 4 costs the most
Rung 01 · zero margin cost

Give documentation, not money

Upgraded cold-chain handling over a stated order value. The full HPLC and mass-spectrometry trace pack rather than the summary. Priority access to the alert list on the next release. A held price for a documented buyer, extended.

Each one deepens the thing the house is actually selling. None of them touches the margin.

Rung 02 · funded, blended

The pair and the set

A leader plus a headroom lot, or two standard lots, priced as one and tested on blended margin. This is the workhorse: it lifts order value, it protects the leader, and it puts the discount where there is room for it.

Rung 03 · retention

The documented-buyer price

A held price on a repeat of the same lot, offered to buyers who have already paid once. Cheaper to serve, no acquisition cost against it, and it never appears on a public page — which is what keeps it from becoming the new list price.

Rung 04 · last resort

Time-boxed lot clearance

One named lot, one dated window, one stated reason — a release moving on. Never a standing rate, never a category, never repeated on the same compound twice in a quarter.

Rung 1 is not a consolation prize. For the buyer this house is built for, a full trace pack and priority on the next release answer the question he is actually asking, which is whether the material is what it says it is. A rupiah discount answers a question he did not ask.

06
Guardrails

Four things that would undo this.

  • A percentage off the whole catalogue. Sixty-nine lots sit within a few points of each other, so a flat rate lands very differently on each and pushes the eight thin lots further under. It also re-prices the book permanently — a market that has seen 15% off waits for 15% off.
  • Discounting a leader to close a deal. BPC-157 at Rp 1.600.000 returns Rp 350.000. Take Rp 200.000 off to win the conversation and the house has worked a full order for Rp 150.000. Pair it instead, every time.
  • A free vial with purchase. Beyond the margin cost, material given away to encourage a larger order reads as encouraging use, which is precisely the line the house does not go near. Give documentation and handling instead.
  • Approving a below-floor order as an exception. The floor holds or it is not a floor. An order that cannot reach 40% blended is re-cut with a different second lot, which is nearly always possible.
