## 1 · OPERATING PROTOCOL (follow in order — do not skip steps)
1. **Load context.** Read §2 (business), §3 (compliance), and §9 (brand appendix) in full
   before writing anything. State back, in five bullets, what AXIOM is and what it may never do.
2. **Spec it.** Invoke gstack **`/spec`** to convert this prompt into a precise, executable
   spec — phases, owners, acceptance criteria. Post the spec before building.
3. **Strategy pass.** Convene **`billion-dollar-board`**. Route each plan section to the right
   expert per §4. Capture each expert's output as its own titled block — do not blend voices.
4. **Model & plan.** Build the business model (§5) and launch roadmap (§6) from the strategy pass.
   All money in **IDR**, formatted `Rp 1.600.000`, every projection carrying stated assumptions.
5. **Pressure-test.** Run gstack **`/plan-ceo-review`** and **`/office-hours`** against the draft.
   Log every objection and either resolve it or record why it stands. A plan that survived no
   scrutiny is not done.
6. **Design the deliverable.** Produce the proposal in the AXIOM identity (§7) using the design
   skills and the tokens in §9. Charts via **`dataviz`**; print output via gstack **`/make-pdf`**.
7. **Verify.** Check every gate in §8 in a real render. Fix what fails. Never report "done" on
   unverified work. Report with the gate table filled in and the PDF path.

**Clarify-or-assume:** if a field is blank or you are told "you decide," adopt the §0 default,
**state the assumption inline**, and proceed. Ask at most **3 sharp questions**, only for
irreversible decisions (legal entity type, capital tier, whether peptides ship at launch).

## 2 · BUSINESS CONTEXT (canonical — extracted from the AXIOM brand guidelines v1.0)
Treat this section as ground truth. If a strategy idea contradicts it, the idea is wrong.

**What AXIOM is.** A human-performance and longevity **house** based in Jakarta, Indonesia —
a **branded house** (one master mark, one standard), not a house of brands. It brings
research-grade peptides, clinical red-light therapy, recovery, wellness and apparel under a
single standard of **verification and documentation**. Direct-to-buyer, bilingual EN/ID, IDR
pricing, WhatsApp-first human support, cold-chain logistics, a Certificate of Analysis per lot.

- **Mission:** make verified, research-grade performance and longevity tools available to people
  who train for decades, not weeks — held to one standard.
- **Positioning:** the considered, clinical-grade house for human performance and longevity in
  Indonesia. *Short-term results, engineered to last.*
- **Brand promise / tagline:** *Human performance, engineered for the long run.*
- **Values:** verification over claims · restraint · the long run · direct.

**The five pillars** (written `AXIOM · Pillar`, never sub-branded):
1. **Peptide Science** — research-grade peptides (metabolic, repair, growth, neuro); every lot
   HPLC/MS verified to ≥ 98%, CoA per lot. **Research Use Only, in-vitro.**
2. **Therapy & Recovery** — clinical red-light therapy at true 660 + 850 nm, verified irradiance;
   compression, mats, masks. Clinical-grade hardware, consumer product.
3. **Wellness** — antioxidants, NAD⁺, cellular-energy support. Consumer product.
4. **Longevity** — cellular-ageing, senescence, copper-peptide compounds for the decade game.
5. **Apparel** — neutral, precise performance wear and everyday carry.

**The four personas** (pain → promise → proof → CTA):
- **The Researcher** (lab buyer/scientist): can't trust identity/purity → verified, documented
  material by lot → HPLC/MS ≥ 98%, CoA, cold-chain → *Request the CoA.*
- **The Decade Athlete** (serious pro, 30–50): hype and neon marketing → a calm clinical system
  for the long game → one standard across every product → *Explore the range.*
- **The Clinic Buyer** (recovery/wellness studio): novelty LEDs, bad specs, unreliable restock →
  clinical-grade, spec-honest hardware → true 660 + 850 nm, verified irradiance → *Talk to the team.*
- **The Identity Wearer** (apparel-first): loud logos that stand for nothing → quiet identity with
  a real standard behind it → same mark, lab to label → *Wear the standard.*

**Commercial mechanics already implied by the brand:** WhatsApp quote-and-close (`+62 812 857 3396`),
itemised IDR quotes with live stock, cold-chain peptide dispatch with CoA in the box, insured/tracked
device shipping, an 18+ / qualified-researcher acknowledgement gate before entry.

## 3 · COMPLIANCE GUARDRAILS (non-negotiable — the credibility *is* the restraint)
The compliance voice is part of the identity, not an appendix. The business plan must treat this
as a **moat**, and every customer-facing artifact the plan proposes must inherit it verbatim.

- **RUO / in-vitro standard.** Peptides are **Research Use Only**, intended exclusively for
  in-vitro laboratory and scientific research. They are **not** drugs, food, cosmetics, or
  supplements, and **not** for human or veterinary consumption, diagnosis, or treatment.
- **Zero medical guidance.** Never provide dosing, administration routes, frequency, cycling,
  stacking, or any medical advice. Describe mechanism, pharmacokinetics and lab handling only.
- **Stated as fact, never buried.** The disclaimer appears wherever peptides appear, set legibly.
- **Acknowledgement gate** before entry: 18+, and for any peptide purchase a qualified researcher
  or institutional buyer.
- **Jurisdiction caution.** Several compounds are controlled/regulated; legality of import,
  possession and handling varies. Buyers are solely responsible for compliance. AXIOM makes no
  representation of legality in any territory.
- **Label rules.** A label *may* state compound, lot, HPLC purity, molecular mass, storage temp;
  *must* carry the RUO micro-disclaimer + tamper-evident seal; *may not* imply human dose, route,
  frequency, cycle, therapeutic benefit, or use "cure/treatment/anti-aging/guaranteed."

The plan must place **regulatory risk as risk #1** and design the go-to-market so that peptides
stay strictly RUO/B2B-research while devices, wellness and apparel carry the consumer growth.

## 5 · PHASE B — BUSINESS MODEL
- **Revenue streams & pricing architecture** per pillar, in IDR (`Rp 1.600.000`). State the
  pricing logic (peptides by lot/purity, devices by spec tier, apparel by line, wellness by SKU).
- **Unit economics** per pillar: COGS (include cold-chain + CoA/verification cost for peptides),
  landed cost, gross margin, CAC by channel, LTV, contribution margin, payback.
- **Supply chain & verification:** sourcing, the HPLC/MS verification partner/pipeline, cold-chain
  (−20 °C storage, 2–8 °C transit), device sourcing and QC, apparel production.
- **Channels:** the site (with acknowledgement gate), WhatsApp commerce, B2B/clinic accounts,
  and how each persona funnel (§4) maps to a channel.
- **Org & capital:** headcount plan by phase, roles (research/QC, ops, support, content),
  capital required per §0 tier, use-of-funds.
- **Financial projection — 3 scenarios** (conservative / base / aggressive): monthly for year 1,
  quarterly for year 2. Revenue by pillar, gross margin, opex, EBITDA, cash runway. Every driver assumption listed.
- **Risk register:** regulatory risk #1 (per §3), then supply/cold-chain, FX (IDR), concentration,
  reputational. Each with likelihood, impact, and mitigation.

## 7 · PHASE D — THE DESIGNED PROPOSAL (brand-locked deliverable)
Produce the plan as a document **in the AXIOM identity**, not a plain report. Use the design
skills (`brandkit` / `high-end-visual-design` / `precision-bento` for layout, `dataviz` for
charts) and the tokens in §9. Non-negotiable brand rules:

- **Palette:** warm near-black canvas `#070605`, bone ink `#F2EDE5`, bronze accent `#C88A4E`
  system. Bronze is **earned emphasis at ≤ 8% of any surface** — never a flood. No purple, no
  cool blue, no pure black/white. Print/PDF inverts to paper `#F2EDE5` on ink, accents in
  `--accent-deep #7C4C24` (core bronze fails small-text contrast on light).
- **Type:** Jost 300 for display/headlines (sentence case, tight leading), Inter for body/UI and
  the wide-tracked uppercase micro-labels. **Tabular numerals for every price, spec and table.**
- **Texture & motion:** hairline dividers as the core texture (never thicken/omit); if animated,
  one signature ease `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`, ≤ 18 px moves, no bounce, reduced-motion static
  equivalent. Static bronze particle-field fallback behind heroes only, behind a scrim.
- **Voice in every line of copy:** proof before promise; short declaratives; quiet confidence;
  honest restraint. **No exclamation marks anywhere.** Use the lexicon (§9); never the avoid-list.
- **Outputs:** (a) an HTML proposal page on the dark canvas, and (b) a print PDF via gstack
  **`/make-pdf`** on the paper palette, with the RUO footer on every peptide-adjacent page.
