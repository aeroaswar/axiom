01Positioning · Godin

Documented, not promised.

One sentence the whole house defends. Own the ground where verification is the product, and open on the smallest market that already believes it.

Positioning statement. AXIOM is the considered, clinical-grade house for human performance and longevity in Indonesia. We bring research-grade peptides, clinical red-light therapy, recovery, wellness and apparel under one standard of verification and documentation. For people who train for decades, not weeks.

The one line. Documented, not promised. Every other message is downstream of it.

The category reframe

The peptide market in Indonesia sells on noise — neon graphics, unverifiable purity, no paperwork, no cold-chain, no person to call. AXIOM does not out-shout it; we change the axis of the decision from how loud to how documented. The competitor sells a vial. We sell a Certificate of Analysis that happens to arrive with a vial.

Smallest viable market — open here
Primary

The Decade Athlete

Serious professional, 30–50, wary of hype, thinks in decades. Wants recovery and longevity tools with real specifications behind them.

Primary

The Clinic Buyer

Recovery / wellness studio sourcing clinical-grade hardware with verified wavelengths and reliable restock.

Secondary

The Researcher

Lab buyer who reads the CoA first and buys by lot. High trust, high reorder, low persuasion cost.

Secondary

The Identity Wearer

Came for the aesthetic, stays for the standard. Top of the funnel, lowest ticket.

We win the first two tribes completely before widening. A market that already distrusts hype does not need to be convinced of restraint — it needs to be found.

Positioning pillars
  • Verification over claims — nothing carries the mark until it is tested, documented and sourced right.
  • Restraint — we state what we can prove and stop. The discipline is the credibility.
  • The long run — built for the decade-over-decade game, not the next wave of attention.
  • Direct — a real line to a real person, before and after the order.
Perceptual map

Two axes decide the category: hype ↔ documented and transactional ↔ relationship. Every loud reseller clusters in hype/transactional. AXIOM stands alone in documented/relationship — the CoA is the documentation, WhatsApp and reorder support are the relationship. That quadrant is empty in Jakarta, and it is defensible because it is expensive to fake.

03Sales · Kennedy + Hormozi

Compete on certainty, not price.

Direct-response selling built around a documentation offer no loud reseller can match, closed by a person over WhatsApp.

The motion. AXIOM sells direct, quote-to-close, over WhatsApp. Content and the site create intent; a person converts it with an itemised quote, live stock and a documentation offer; the relationship is retained with reorder prompts and a fresh CoA each time.

Three sales motions
  • Inbound WhatsApp — demand created by content and the catalogue, closed by quote. The default engine.
  • Outbound B2B — clinic and studio prospecting for the device and recurring-supply tier. High ticket, high retention.
  • Reorder & retention — documented buyers are prompted for the same lot with a new CoA. The cheapest revenue in the business.
The offer — assembled, per pillar

Each pillar leads with a documentation-first offer. The value is not a discount; it is certainty, assembled from parts a reseller does not carry.

Pricing posture

Prices are held at a 53% gross margin (cost basis = supplier lot + Rp 600.000, rounded to the nearest Rp 100.000). We do not chase the lowest number in the market — a lower price on undocumented material is a different product. The quote always names the proof, then the price.

Objection framework
  • Price → reframe to documentation and support; the money-back documentation promise removes the downside.
  • Trust → send a representative CoA; let the paperwork answer.
  • Stock → reserve the lot; name the honest count remaining.
  • Timing → hold the quote and the price for a stated window; make waiting safe, not urgent-by-trick.

06Closing Scripts · Belfort

The words that close, on brand.

WhatsApp scripts for greeting, quote, the three objections, reserve, reorder and close.

Warm, proof-led, plain-spoken. Contractions are allowed on WhatsApp. The line always names the proof before the price, and never provides dosing, routes, frequency or any medical guidance.

Straight line — three certainties to build
  • The material — the CoA, HPLC/MS ≥ 98%, cold-chain.
  • The house — documentation, insured shipping, a person who answers.
  • You — a documented track record the buyer can verify.
Greeting

Hi — thanks for reaching out to AXIOM. Happy to help. Tell me what you’re looking to source and I’ll send an itemised quote with live stock and IDR pricing.

Quote delivery

Here’s your quote. Everything’s HPLC/MS verified to ≥ 98% with a Certificate of Analysis in the box, shipped cold-chain, with BAC water and a research kit included.

Reply here and I’ll get it moving.

Price objection

I hear you on price. What you’re paying for is documentation — a CoA per lot, verified purity, cold-chain, and a real person before and after the order.

If the paperwork ever doesn’t match, it’s on us. Want me to hold your lot at this price?

Trust objection

Fair question. Every lot ships with its Certificate of Analysis — HPLC and mass spectrometry, ≥ 98%.

I can send a representative CoA now so you can see exactly what you’re getting.

Stock / reserve

That lot’s moving — I’ve got a limited count left at this price. Want me to reserve yours while you decide? No pressure either way.

Reorder prompt

Your last documented order was GHK-Cu, back in the spring. Want me to prep the same again with a fresh CoA? I can have it dispatched today.

Close

Great — I’ll confirm the order and CoA, and it moves to dispatch cold-chain, insured and tracked. Thanks for choosing AXIOM.

Script complianceNo script states or implies a human dose, route, frequency, repeat schedule or therapeutic benefit. Peptides are Research Use Only, in-vitro. Where a buyer asks for usage guidance, the reply is that AXIOM provides material and documentation only, and does not advise on use.

The founder’s daily, weekly, monthly and quarterly rhythm — energy and decisions engineered like the product.

Decision routine
  • Reversible decisions are made fast and logged; the day is not spent deliberating what can be undone.
  • Irreversible decisions — a supplier, a compliance line, a price architecture — go through the standard: proof first, then sign-off, then ship.
  • Compliance-adjacent calls default to caution: when unsure, say less and route it to review.
Energy as a system

The brand is built for the decade game; the founder’s rhythm has to be too. Protected sales blocks, a fixed weekly review, and a real recovery practice keep the operator documented and steady — the same standard applied to the person that the CoA applies to the lot.

As the house scales
  • The daily log becomes a shared dashboard; the counts stay the same.
  • The weekly review adds the first ops hire; the monthly review adds content and B2B owners.
  • Sign-off matrix: the brand owner approves identity and voice; compliance approves every peptide-adjacent surface; either can block release.
