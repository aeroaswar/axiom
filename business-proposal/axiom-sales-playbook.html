01
Hormozi · the offer

The first order is the hard one. Make it obvious.

A serious buyer facing Rp 2.800.000 of material from a house he has not bought from is not weighing price. He is weighing whether the paperwork is real. Assemble the answer to that, name it, and put it on the table before he asks.

Repairs — order value, and part of quote → close

The value equation, read correctly for AXIOM

The equation is dream outcome, multiplied by the likelihood it is achieved, divided by time and effort. For a compliant house the dream outcome is certainty of material — identity, purity, chain of custody, a person who answers. It is never a health result, and stating it as one would cost more than it earns.

The assembled offer — built from what the house already carries

Nothing below is a new cost. Every component already exists in the operation and is simply unnamed and unpriced in the conversation, which is the same as being invisible. Name it, assign it a place in the offer, and state it in the same order every time.

Core

The documented lot

Research-grade material, HPLC/MS verified to ≥ 98%, Certificate of Analysis for that specific lot in the box and sent by message on dispatch.

Assembled · 01

Assembled · 02

Assembled · 03

Assembled · 04

Assembled · 05

The held reorder price

A documented buyer’s price is held for 90 days on the same compound, so the second order needs no negotiation.

The three-part guarantee

Risk reversal here can only ever cover documentation, handling and supply. It can never cover an outcome, and the restraint is what makes it credible — a house that will not promise a result but will refund a missing document is telling the buyer exactly what it controls.

  • Documentation. A Certificate of Analysis for your lot, in the box. If it is missing or does not match the material described, the order is refunded in full.
  • Cold chain. Material leaves at 2–8 °C, insured and tracked. If it arrives outside the stated handling condition, it is replaced from the next lot at no charge.
  • Supply. Your documented price is held for 90 days on the same compound. If a lot you have ordered before is out, you are on the alert list for its release before it is offered generally.
Compliance gate on this sectionEvery element above is a guarantee of documentation, handling or supply. No component of the offer states or implies a human dose, route, frequency, repeat schedule, therapeutic benefit or any outcome. Peptides remain Research Use Only, in-vitro.

The single-vial reflex, and the arithmetic against it

A buyer asks about one compound and receives a price for one compound. That is not a decision the house has influenced — it is order-taking. The alternative is to quote the single lot and one assembled set beside it, on the same message, with the documentation identical in both. Composition of a set reflects lot release and documentation only; it carries no implication about use, and the RUO line travels with it.

02
Belfort · the close

Three certainties, and the two questions that find the weak one.

A buyer who does not order is not short of information. He is short of certainty in one of three places, and answering the wrong one loses the conversation politely. Diagnose first, then loop.

Repairs — conversation → quote, quote → close
Certainty 01

The material

Is this what it says it is. Answered with the CoA, the HPLC/MS figure, the lot number and the cold chain — never with an adjective.

Certainty 02

The house

Will this arrive, documented, and will they be here next year. Answered with dispatch photographs, tracking, restock history and the standard.

Certainty 03

The person

Is there someone accountable. Answered by being fast, named, plain-spoken, and by saying what AXIOM does not do.

The two-question diagnostic

Run this before any objection handling. It costs one message and tells you which certainty is low, which decides everything that follows.

Diagnostic

YouHappy to help. Two quick things so I quote you properly — which compound and lot size are you after, and have you sourced documented material before, or would this be your first time working with a documented supplier?

First half qualifies the order. Second half surfaces the real state: a buyer who has bought documented material before needs the material certainty answered. A buyer who has not needs the house and the person answered first, and the CoA will not do it on its own.

Five loops

A loop does not argue with the objection. It isolates it, raises the certainty underneath it, and returns to the same question. Three loops in one conversation is normal here and is not pressure — each one adds information the buyer did not have.

Loop 01 · price

ThemIt is more expensive than what I have been quoted elsewhere.

You — isolateThat is fair, and probably true. Can I ask — if the price were the same, would you take the documented one, or is there something else in the way?

You — raiseHere is the difference in what you receive. Every lot is HPLC and mass-spec verified to ≥ 98%, and the Certificate of Analysis for your specific lot is in the box and in your chat. It moves cold-chain, insured and tracked. If the documentation is missing or does not match, the order is refunded. A cheaper vial without paperwork is a different product, not the same product at a lower number.

You — returnWant me to send a representative CoA so you can see exactly what arrives, and hold your lot at this price while you look at it?

Loop 02 · is this legitimate

ThemHow do I know the material is real.

You — acknowledgeThe right question, and the reason the house exists.

You — raiseI will send you a Certificate of Analysis from a recent lot now — compound, lot number, HPLC purity, mass confirmation, storage temperature. Yours arrives the same way, for your lot, before you pay anything. On dispatch you get the photograph of your parcel and the tracking number.

You — returnHave a look at it, and tell me if that is the level of documentation you are working to.

Send the document, not a claim about the document. The paperwork answers faster than any sentence.

Loop 03 · stock and timing

ThemLet me come back to you next week.

You — isolateNo problem. Is it the timing on your side, or are you still deciding on the material.

You — raiseTwo honest things about next week. I hold your price for seven days, so waiting costs you nothing there. What I cannot hold is the lot — there are a limited number of units left from this release, and the next one is not dated yet.

You — returnI can reserve yours now at this price with no obligation, and release it if you decide against. Want me to do that.

Only ever state a count you can verify. A reserve that is not real is the one thing that destroys the standard faster than a bad lot.

Loop 04 · the legality question

ThemIs this allowed here.

YouStraight answer. Material is supplied as Research Use Only, for in-vitro laboratory research — not for human or veterinary use, and we provide no guidance on use. Several compounds are regulated differently in different jurisdictions, and confirming what applies to you is your responsibility as the buyer. I would rather tell you that plainly than have you find it later.

Never soften this and never improvise around it. Buyers who want an evasive answer are not buyers the house can keep, and the restraint itself closes the serious ones.

Loop 05 · the usage question

ThemHow much should I use, and how often.

YouI have to be straight with you — AXIOM supplies material and documentation only, and does not advise on use in any form. What I can tell you is what is in the vial, the purity figure, the mass confirmation and how it is stored and shipped. Want me to send the CoA for the lot.

This is the single most common message the house will receive and the single most expensive one to answer badly. The reply is fixed, it is never abbreviated, and it is never given by voice note where it cannot be quoted back accurately.

Tonality, for a channel that has no voice

  • Certainty on the material. Short declaratives, figures, no hedging words. “Verified to ≥ 98%. CoA in the box.”
  • The reasonable register on price. Longer sentences, acknowledgement first. This is the only place contractions and warmth do real work.
  • Plain and slow on compliance. Full sentences, no abbreviation, in text and never in a voice note.
  • Voice notes for one thing only — the greeting and the human moment. Numbers, specifications and compliance stay in text where they can be read back.
  • No exclamation marks anywhere. The house does not raise its voice, on any channel.
03
Kennedy · the asset and the ladder

The quote is the sales letter. The ladder is the revenue.

Direct response has always been one structure and one discipline: an asset that does the selling on its own, and a sequence that keeps arriving with something new. AXIOM has the first in rough form and does not have the second at all. This is the largest single leak in the house.

Repairs — quote → close, order → reorder

The quote, rebuilt on structure

Same six blocks, same order, every time. Proof before price, one action at the end, nothing else asked of the reader.

  1. The proof line. One sentence naming what makes this material documented, before any number appears.
  2. The itemisation. Compound, lot size, unit price, quantity, line total — tabular, in IDR, no rounding surprises.
  3. What is in the box. CoA for your lot, BAC water and research kit, cold-chain dispatch, insured tracking.
  4. The three-part guarantee. Documentation, cold chain, supply — stated in one line each, verbatim from §01.
  5. The held window. A real date. “This price is held until Friday 12 September.”
  6. One action. A single instruction. Confirm and it moves to dispatch. Never two options, never a question that can be answered with silence.

The quote is sent as one message, not five. Fragmented quotes read as improvised and invite a fragmented reply.

The follow-up ladder

Six touches over thirty days, and each one carries a named asset rather than a nudge. A nudge asks the buyer to do work. An asset gives him a reason to reopen the conversation, and it is the reason, not the reminder, that closes the 42 quotes currently going quiet.

The thirtieth touch is a close, not a farewell. A buyer who declines six times and joins the alert list has converted from a lost quote into a standing audience for every future release — which is the same asset the content engine is trying to build, acquired for free at the end of a conversation that was going to end anyway.

The reorder engine

The cheapest revenue in the business is a documented buyer being asked a second time. It currently depends on the buyer remembering. Two mechanisms fix that, and both are calendar work rather than marketing spend.

  • The 90-day prompt. Every paid order creates one dated task at ninety days. The message names the compound and the lot, offers the held price, and offers a fresh CoA. It is a single message, sent by a person.
  • The lot-release broadcast. When a lot is verified and released, the alert list for that compound hears first — with the CoA attached, the count available, and the price. One broadcast per release, never a general newsletter.
90-day reorder prompt

Your last documented order was GHK-Cu 50 mg, lot released in June. A new lot is verified and in stock, CoA attached. Your price is held at what you paid. Want me to prepare the same again, or would you rather see what else came out of this release.

Lot-release broadcast

New lot released — BPC-157 10 mg. HPLC and mass-spec verified to ≥ 98%, CoA attached. Limited units from this release, cold-chain dispatch as usual. Reply here for an itemised quote.

Broadcast copy carries the RUO line wherever a compound is named. No superlatives, no exclamation marks, no reference to use.

05
Vaynerchuk · demand

The verification work is the content.

Content is inconsistent for the same reason revenue is: it is treated as a separate job requiring a separate mood. It is not. Every lot that clears verification generates a week of material as a by-product, and the only discipline required is capturing it while the work happens.

Repairs — upstream volume

One lot, one week

When a lot is verified, three minutes of capture at the bench produces everything below. Shoot first, decide later — the material is worthless once the parcel has left.

  1. Capture at verification. The CoA on the bench, the trace on screen, the vial and label, the cold-chain packing, the parcel leaving. Five stills and two short clips.
  2. Publish the release. The CoA post, the anchor for the week, carrying the lot number and the purity figure.
  3. Cut the week. Trace detail, label macro, packing sequence, dispatch — four further posts from the same three minutes.
  4. Write the teardown. One standard explained in depth, published weekly, and cut into the following week’s short posts.
  5. Broadcast it. The same release goes to the alert list as a quote invitation, which is where content becomes pipeline.

Caption rules

  • Every caption carries a number, a method or a document. If it carries none of the three, it is not a proof post and it does not go out.
  • No superlatives, no exclamation marks, no comparison to a named competitor.
  • Mechanism and laboratory handling only. Never dose, route, frequency, repeat schedule or benefit.
  • Any post naming a compound carries the Research Use Only, in-vitro line. Without exception, including stories.
  • The call to action is always the same one: a message for an itemised quote.
Content compliance gate — before publishingDoes the caption state or imply a human dose, route, frequency, repeat schedule or therapeutic benefit. Does it use a superlative or an exclamation mark. Does it name a compound without the Research Use Only, in-vitro line. Three answers of no, and it ships.
06
Godin & Robbins · spread and rhythm

Make the document travel. Make the day repeat.

Two questions decide whether this compounds: what a satisfied buyer can hand to a training partner, and what the founder does on the days when nothing is urgent.

Repairs — upstream volume, order → reorder, consistency

The forwardable artifact

Nobody forwards a price. People forward a document that makes them look careful. The Certificate of Analysis is already the most remarkable object the house produces — it simply needs to be built to travel: the mark on it, the lot legible, the purity figure unmissable, a clean file name, and a single line at the foot naming where it came from and the compliance standard it was issued under.

That is the whole referral mechanism. A buyer sends a friend the paperwork, not a pitch, and the paperwork is the argument. It is also the one piece of marketing a competitor selling undocumented material cannot copy at any price, which is what makes it worth the effort of designing properly.

  • Design the CoA as a brand surface — on the AXIOM standard, not the supplier’s letterhead alone.
  • Name the file so it survives forwarding — compound, lot, date. It will be re-sent months later with no message attached.
  • Ask once, at the right moment. On the dispatch confirmation, not at the sale: “If anyone asks where it came from, the CoA has everything they need.”

The weekly rhythm

Inconsistent revenue is almost always an inconsistent week. The fix is not motivation, it is a fixed shape that survives a bad mood, a slow month and a travel week.

Execution

The thirty-day sprint. What ships, in what order.

Week one is measurement and assets, because everything after depends on a real baseline. No new traffic is bought until the ladder exists — buying attention for a funnel that leaks at the second junction is the most expensive mistake available.

Week 1 · measure and arm
Re-base the model and build the two assets
Count the real numbers out of the message history — conversations, quotes, orders, reorders — and overwrite every figure in §00 and §04. Write the quote template (§03) and the six ladder assets. Design the CoA to travel (§06). Fix the five loop scripts (§02) where they can be copied, not improvised.
Week 2 · the ladder, retroactively
Run every open and dead quote through the ladder
Every quote sent in the last ninety days that never closed enters at the day-appropriate rung. This is the single highest-return week in the sprint: the conversations already exist, they cost nothing to reopen, and the asset carried is new information rather than a reminder. Capture at the bench becomes routine; the weekly teardown ships; the alert list is offered at the end of every ladder. Close the month against the scoreboard, re-base the assumptions again, and change one thing.

08
Synthesis

Eight views, one instruction.

Answer the second message, and ask the second time.

The board converges on an unglamorous conclusion. AXIOM does not have a positioning problem, a pricing problem or a product problem — the strategy already solved those, and the standard is genuinely defensible. It has a sequence problem. A quote is sent once, an order is taken once, and a buyer is asked once. Every leak on the map is a version of the same omission, and each one is repaired with words and a calendar rather than capital.

So the month is spent on the second touch, not on new attention: the assembled offer beside every single, six ladder assets behind every quote, a ninety-day prompt behind every order, and a proof post behind every verification. Volume applies to the pipeline. The claim stays exactly where the standard put it.
