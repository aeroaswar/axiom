# Margin structure — supplier cost, base price, selling price, margin

**The Rp 600.000 on every lot is the pen** — R-Peptides' *Reusable Injection Pen V2*,
listed at exactly Rp 600.000 on their supplies sheet. The supplier ships **vials
only**; AXIOM buys a pen separately and sells the two together. BAC water and the
research kit are free inclusions on top. Because the pen is a bought good, what
remains is a **true gross margin**.

```
supplier cost  (vial)
+ pen          Rp 600.000
= base price
selling price − base price = margin
```

Presented in the AXIOM house design at `axiom-margin-structure.html`.
Per-lot data: `data/axiom-margin-structure.csv`.

## What this does not measure

Net margin. Operating cost — premises, staff, marketing, payment fees, wastage, tax
— appears in neither the price list nor the supplier sheet. **123.080.000 of gross
margin on 249.800.000 of revenue** is the envelope all of it has to come out of.
